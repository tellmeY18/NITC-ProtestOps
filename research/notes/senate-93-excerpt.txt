S93.3.3 Proposal from Dean (Student Welfare) on SAC Constitution Committee Report
Background:
Dean (Students Welfare) has proposed revising the Student Affairs Council (SAC) constitution based on
the report submitted by the Constitution Redrafting Committee appointed by him. It may be noted that
the SAC constitution was originally drafted in 2008. The committee has put forth several
recommendations, which include the following key points: 7

1. The Joint Secretary (JS) position will serve as the intermediary between the Dean Students' Welfare
(DSW) office and the Executive Committee (EC). The JS will be responsible for coordinating the
activities of the institute.
2. A new position, known as the Finance Secretary (FS), to be introduced. The FS will be in charge of
managing the finances of the students' general body and overseeing the expenditures incurred by the
executive wings of the GSB.
3. To ensure adequate representation of all academic levels within the EC and Senate, a new position called
the PG Academic Secretary is to be introduced. The PG Academic Secretary will be responsible for
advocating for the interests of postgraduate students during elections.
4. The Alumni and Placements Affairs Secretary will be responsible for coordinating all alumni-related
activities. Additionally, this position will be a member of the institute’s Placement Council, overseeing
the coordination of activities conducted by the Centre for Career Development (CCD)
Decision:
The following recommendations were considered and approved.
1. The inclusion of the Finance Secretary and the PG Academic Affairs Secretary as part of the new EC
positions. Additionally, the revised responsibilities for the Joint Secretary are accepted. The chairman
of the senate recommended dividing the proposed Alumni and Placement Secretary position into two
distinct roles: Alumni Affairs Secretary and Career and Placement Affairs Secretary.
2. During the discussion, a newly suggested clause aimed at ensuring representation from all
demographics, female students and Socially and Economically Disadvantaged Groups (SEDGs) was
brought up. The chairman of the senate proposed that seats should be randomly reserved to guarantee
equitable representation. The senators agreed and it was resolved to randomly reserve seats based on
gender, home state/non-home state, and SEDGs.
3. The proposed advisory committee under the executive council, along with its responsibilities, was
approved without any objections.
4. The new clause allowing re-election to any vacant post was passed by the senate.
5. The CGPA requirement for contesting elections was revised to 6.5.
6. To ensure the candidate has no disciplinary actions against him/her, the nomination sheet must be
recommended by the Head of Department concerned and the Chief Warden.
7. All members of the senate unanimously agreed to the newly proposed amendments to the speaker’s
responsibilities.
8. Each department's Ph.D. scholars will be represented by a counsellor.

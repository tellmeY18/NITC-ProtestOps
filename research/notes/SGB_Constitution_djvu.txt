Student Governance Body 


Introduction 


Student Governance Body (SGB) is a student body of institute that enhances the student experience 
and fostering a vibrant, inclusive, and supportive campus environment. It acts as the representative 
body of the student community to bring forth duly the requirements of the student to the authorities. It 
also provides a platform for the students to tone up their leadership and communication skills. It shall 


organize activities for the overall development and academic excellence of students. 


This constitution aims at providing the students opportunities for overall personality development, with 
emphasis on leadership qualities and the spirit of service based on the spirit ofmutual co-operation 
among the various sections of the campus community in the National Institute of Technology Calicut 
is proposed in the pages to follow, including the bye-laws of the various Councils, responsibilities and 


functions of the representatives and the framework for the election of candidates to the Council.

PREAMBLE 


For the purpose of 


Providing the students opportunities for overall personality development, based on equality of 


status and sound moral judgement; 


Developing all round extra - curricular activities to supplement education with emphasis on 
leadership qualities and the spirit of service; 

and 
based on the spirit of mutual co-operation among the various sections of the campus 


community; 


We, the students of National Institute of Technology Calicut resolve to constitute a democratic 


organization called Student Governance Body and do here by adopt this Constitution. 


CHAPTER I 


The structure of this Constitution has been expressed in the formation of Executive Council (EC), and 
Student Governance Body (SGB). The nature of their interaction with each other and with the 
General Student Body (GSB) is defined below. 


All registered students of the Institute shall be members of the GSB and shall constitute its general 
body. 


The Director of the Institute shall be the Patron of the GSB. 


The EC will be headed by the Dean Student Welfare (DSW), who will be the Rector of SGB. The 
SGB will be governed by the EC. It will be managed by the students under the guidance of EC 


members. 


The SGB shall function as a student body that executes the student activities. The SGB can also 


proposes the policies based on the demands and aspirations of the GSB. 
The EC can put forth suggestions to, and advise the SGB as and when it deems necessary. 


The functional manifestation of the spirit of the Constitution depends mainly on the integrity and the 


wisdom of the SGB Councilors and the Executives. 


The various sections of the campus community shall, together, strive towards building an 
atmosphere imbued with warmth and the spirit of objective analysis, to attain the culture of a 


truly educated community.

. Office Bearers of the SGB 


A. Speaker 

a) The Speaker shall be the Presiding Officer on the floor of the SGB and he or she shall be from 
the SGB members and elected by the Councilors of the SGB. He or she cannot hold any elected 
post outside the SGB. 


b) Duties: 

1. The Speaker shall convene and conduct the meetings of the SGB. 

2. The Speaker shall normally prepare the agenda for every SGB meeting and shall inform the 
members, the EC and the GSB at least 8 hours in advance, except for Special Meetings (SM) 
(Ref: Article 5 J of this chapter) or Emergency Meetings (EM) (Ref: Articles5 Q, R,S of this 
chapter). 

3. The Speaker shall prepare and be responsible for communicating the approved minutes of each 
Regular SGB Meeting (RM) to the GSB within four days after approval. The minutes of a Special 
Meeting shall be put forward to the GSB within the next three days. 

4. The Speaker shall send the minutes of Regular Meetings to all Councilors within three days of 
the meetings and if, within four days of the despatch, no objection is received, the minutes shall 
be treated as approved 

5. The Speaker shall be responsible for the maintenance of the following documents and for keeping 
them up to date: 

e The minutes of the SGB sessions held in the immediate past five years. 

e The reports of all committees, sub-committees, Institute and Editorial Boards and 
executivessubmitted in the past five years. 

e All correspondence from any individual or group of individuals of the GSB for the past 
fiveyears. 

e Acopy of the Constitution and amendments. 

6. The Speaker shall be responsible for the production of the above documents when demanded by 

any of the following: 

e Councilors of the SGB within 72 hours 


e The Faculty members of SGB within 24 hours 


e Members of the EC within 48 hours 

e Campus Press within 72 hours 

e The representatives of at least 50 signatories of the GSB within 4 days 

e Any other person or body authorized by SGB within 5 days. 

7. The Speaker shall keep a check on the membership of the Councilors and the Executives tothe 
various committees, sub-committees, boards, etc. 

8. The Speaker shall have a copy of the Constitution and its amendments during every SGBmeeting 
and Emergency Council meeting. 

9. The Speaker shall call for, compile and release to the GSB reports from: 

e Individual student members of the EC, covering the activities of the various 
subcommittees, Institute Standing Committees, Campus Editorial Board, Hostel 
representatives, SGB, SGB sub- Committees 

e This should be done twice every year, in the first weeks of December and April. 

10. The Speaker shall be fully conversant with the Constitution, and shall, at the end of hisor her 


tenure, recommend a course of action to his her successor.

a) The DS shall be nominated by the Speaker subject to ratification by the SGB. The DS isa

e The DS shall assist the Speaker in all matters concerning the SGB

. Rules and Procedures in the SGB 
All the SGB meetings shall be Regular Meetings unless declared Special Meetings. 


A. Order of Transactions 


a) The Speaker shall take items in the following order while conducting SGB meetings: 
1. Ratification of draft minutes if necessary 


2. Announcements 


3. Unfinished transactions 


4. Reports of the Executives 


5. New t transactions on the Agenda 


6. Any item not on the Agenda with the support of one-third of the student floor strength of the 
SGB or at least 10% of the GSB. 
b) The order of transactions may be modified by a simple majority of the SGB. 


B. Frequency of SGB meetings 


The interval between two successive meetings shall not exceed five weeks so as to makeup a 
minimum of three meetings a semester, starting from the day of enrolment. The meetingsshall be 
distributed evenly throughout the semester. 


C. Convening of extraordinary SGB meetings 
The Speaker shall convene Regular Meetings within a week of a requisition in writing by: 


1. At least one-third the strength of SGB and with the presence of two Faculty Members or 
2. At least a minimum of 10% of the existing strength of GSB and with the presence of two 
Faculty Members. 
D. Quorum 


a) The quorum for an RM of the SGB shall be half the current strength of the SGB 


b) The quorum for any SM of the SGB shall be two-thirds of the current strength of the SGB 

c) If the floor strength of a SGB meeting falls below the specified quorum during the meeting,any 
decision taken thereafter will not be valid. 

E. Attendance 

Whenever a Councilor expects to be away from the campus or be unavailable for a periodof 
ten days or more, the Councilor shall intimate the Speaker in writing, in advance. However, in cases 
of emergency, he or she should inform the Speaker in writing within five days of the commencement 
of his or her absence. If any Councilor fails to attend two consecutive meetings and/or a total of 
three meetings a semester without prior information and/or explanation, then the defaulter stands 
automatically expelled from the SGB. 

A written explanation for absence from a SGB meeting shall normally be passed on to the 
Speaker within five days after the SGB meeting by the absentee. The Speaker shall read out the 
explanation in the next SGB meeting and the SGB may accept or reject the explanation bya simple 
majority. The Speaker shall notify in writing, the defaulter about the decision on expulsion within 


five days of the expulsion and inform the GSB through the minutes. 
F. Presence of an individual of the GSB in the RM 
Any member of the GSB can attend the RM. 


G. Presence of the EC in the RM 


The Student Executives shall attend all the SGB meetings but may absent themselves by 
informing to the Speaker. They will state the reasons to the Speaker in writing, who in turn will seek 
the approval of the SGB. In such an eventuality, the Executive should depute a member of the GSB 
(who is not a Councilor/EC member) to represent him or her on the floor of the SGB. The Executives 
shall be answerable to the GSB with regard to the non-attendanceof SGB meetings. 

Note : An individual, a representative of a group of students, an Executive, or any other person who 


has been allowed to attend the RM, can put forth his/her views on the floor of the SGB. 


H. Special Meeting (SM) of the SGB 

a) No member of the GSB shall normally be permitted to attend the SM. 

b) The Executive Council and the Faculty Members are free to attend the SM. 

c) The Speaker may exclude any student from the SM in case discussion pertains to thatperson 

d) A simple majority of the SGB shall decide whether the concerned student or any other students 
from the GSB may be permitted to attend the SM. 

e) SM convened in advance — A simple majority of the floor strength of that shall decidewhether 
the next meeting should be a SM. 

f) SM convened during an RM — A simple majority of the floor strength of the SGB shalldecide 


whether the rest of a session should be a SM. 


I. Misbehavior on the floor of the SGB 
In the event of misbehavior by any member of the SGB, the student Executives, the GSB, the 
CP during a session, the Speaker shall have the right to suspend the student from that session. Also, 


repeated misbehavior or two suspensions can lead to expulsion. 


J. Motions in the SGB Meetings 

Any motion can be moved only with the permission of the Speaker. 

a) Adjournment Motion 

1. An adjournment motion may be moved by any member of the SGB while the session is in 
progress. 

2. The Speaker shall put the adjournment motion to vote immediately after proposal. 

3. Adjournment motions shall be passed by a simple majority of the SGB. 

b) Censure Motion 
The censure motions can be passed against the Councilors, the Speaker, the student Executives 
and others accountable to the SGB. 

1. A censure motion shall be submitted in writing to the Speaker at least two days in advance. 

2. In case the Speaker is the defendant, he or she shall vacate the chair and the DS shall chairthe 

session. If the DS is the defendant, he shall vacate the chair and the Speaker shall appoint aCouncilor 

to act as the DS for the session. 


3. All censure motions shall be duly proposed in writing and shall be seconded by at least one 


Councilor in writing, this Councilor being other than the proposer 
4. A censure motion may be discussed in the absence of the defendant. 
5. No censure motion shall be put to vote unless the defendant has been given adequate opportunity 


to defend himself or herself before the SGB, and an adequate discussion has beenhad. 


K. Overruling the decision of the Speaker. 
Any decision of the Speaker can be overruled by a two-thirds majority of the floor strength of the 
SGB. 
L. Final session of the SGB 
The final session of the SGB for a particular year shall be a joint session of the old andthe 

new SGB s and ECs. The SGB and the EC completing their tenure through the Speaker present an 
account of the activities during their tenure, hand all records and charge, and shallsuggest a future 
course of action to their successors. 
M. Tenure of the SGB 

The term of office of the members of the SGB (including the Faculty Members), and the EC 
shall be one year. (Ref: Chap VII, 4 A d). 
N. The Emergency Council (ECL) 
a) The ECL shall be a sub-council of the SGB formed during the last meeting of the SGBbefore 
every vacation, to represent the SGB on issues which require immediate attentionduring the 
vacation. 


b) The ECL shall consist of: 
- The Speaker/DS as convener 


- The GS or one other EC member nominated by the GS 

- Five councilors representing diverse interests suggested by the Speaker and ratified bythe SGB. 
c) The ECL is free to enlist the assistance of any students. However, these students shall haveno 
vote in the SGB and shall not be accountable to the SGB for the action of the EC. 
d) The ECL shall consult the EC member concerned, regarding matters under the purview of a 
particular Executive. 
e) The ECL is not empowered to take decisions on far reaching policy matters. The ECL shallgather 
information regarding all issues and forward its recommendations to the SGB. Such 
recommendations may or may not be ratified by the SGB. 
f) The minutes of the ECL meetings shall not be made public unless and until they have been 
ratified by the SGB. 
g) The members of the ECL should make themselves readily available so that an ECL meeting can 


be convened at short notice. 


O. Emergency Meeting (EM) of the SGB during vacation 


The speaker is empowered to convene an EM of the SGB during the vacation giving three 
weeks’ notice, should the need for such a meeting arise, to discuss issues of far reaching importance. 
P. Emergency Meeting of the SGB during a semester 

The Speaker is empowered to convene an EM of the SGB during the semester with a minimum 
notice of 8 hours, if urgent matters arise. Alternatively, the speaker may proceed with the meeting 
upon approval from the faculty coordinators or the DSW office to address issues of significant 
importance. 

Q. Finance Committee (FC) 

The Primary aim of the Finance Committee is to manage the finances of the Students’ 
General Body, and it shall control and supervise the expenditures incurred by the executive 
wings of the GSB. It shall report to the SGB of Finances and Accounts periodically, as 
prescribed by the SGB 
1. It consists of three members including its Convener. 

2. All members of FC will be SGB members and one of them will be elected as convener ofthe 
FC by the SGB. 

3. The FC will function as a team, but for administrative convenience the responsibilitiescan be 
distributed. 

4. Every FC Member will be an ex-officio member of the council/committee he is attachedto 
and the convener FC shall be ex-officio member of all councils/ committees. 

5. Finance Committee will finalize the budget on the recommendation and requirements of 
various councils/committees and will present to the SGB for ratification. 

6. In the budgets presented by the councils/committees, separate heads and allocation areto be 
made for various club activities, other major expenses and estimates of miscellaneous 
expenses. 

7. If the SGB advises some changes in the budget of any council/committee the corresponding 
executive shall meet his council/committee members to modify thebudget proposal, which 
he or she will put to SGB for ratification. 

8. The FC shall submit regular reports (at least once in three months) on the status of SGBfinance 


at the moment. 


9. The finance committee will present its final report before the joint session of the SGB.This 
report must include : 
(a) A balance sheet (of income /expenditure) 
(b) Any exceptional expenditure 
(c) Recommended action. 


10. Written annual report of the treasurer, checked by the audit board, constituted by the DOS should 


be circulated to the students.

CHAPTER VI 


AMENDMENTS TO THE CONSTITUTION 


1. A. There shall be no amendments to any particular clause of the Constitution for 2 years fromthe 


date of its approval from the Director, NIT Calicut. 


B. Amendments after this period 

a. Initiated by the GSB: An amendment to any clause of this Constitution can be initiated if 
more than one-fourth of the total strength of the GSB seeks it through a signed memorandum 
addressed to the DSW. The DSW shall take final decision. The DSW may seek the opinion of GSB 
through the Speaker if needed. 


b. Initiated by the SGB: An amendment to any clause of this Constitution can be initiated if 
more than half the total strength of SGB and two faculty members seeks it through a signed 
memorandum addressed to the DSW. The DSW shall take final decision. The DSW may seek the 
opinion of GSB through the Speaker if needed. 


c. Initiated by a Faculty members of SGB /EC: A Faculty member of SGB/EC who wishes to 
amend any clause of this Constitution shall put forward his proposal to the Dean — Students in 
writing. The DSW shall take final decision. The DSW may seek the opinion of GSB through the 
Speaker if needed. 


2. Amendment Procedure 

A. The Speaker shall inform the GSB of the amendment 72 hours of the receipt of the signed 
memorandum, and shall request the GSB to give their opinions/suggestions, if any, on the 
proposed amendment within one week. 

B. The Speaker shall convene a RM of the SGB within 3 weeks of the date of receipt of thesigned 
memorandum, in order to discuss the proposed amendment. 

C. a) In the case of a memorandum initiated by the GSB, the Speaker shall conduct a referendum 
within 7 days of the discussion on the proposed amendment, after stating thefinal version(s) of 
the amendment to the GSB. 

b) The referendum shall be considered valid if and only if at least one-third of the GSB voteon 
it. 

c) The proposed amendment shall be put forward to the through DSW only if a simple majority 
votesin favour of it. 


d) The DSW shall take final decision and shall forward to the Director for approval 


3. Amendments to this chapter (Chapter VIII) of the Constitution can only be made on asigned 
requisition initiated by the GSB. (Read Clause 1 B(a) above) 


4. NOTE TO STUDENTS 
Notwithstanding any of the provisions of any of the above clauses of this Chapter, amendments 
to this Constitution shall come into force only after the Director, NIT Calicut approves the 


amendments.

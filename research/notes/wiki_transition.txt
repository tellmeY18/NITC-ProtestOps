This page documents the key differences between the '''Students Affairs Council (SAC) Constitution''' (2008) and the '''Student Governance Body (SGB) Constitution''' that replaced it, serving as a reference for understanding the transition in student governance at NIT Calicut.

== Overview of Changes ==

The transition from SAC to SGB represents a fundamental restructuring of student governance at NIT Calicut. The most significant change is the shift from a '''student-led autonomous model''' (SAC as the supreme policy-making body with the EC as its subordinate executing arm) to a '''faculty-guided governance model''' (SGB managed by students under the guidance and governance of the EC, which includes faculty members and is headed by the DSW).

== Key Differences in Roles and Responsibilities ==

=== Speaker ===
* '''SAC:''' Elected by SAC councilors; must be from GSB and NOT a SAC Councilor; informs GSB 48 hours in advance of meetings; document production to representatives of 25 signatories
* '''SGB:''' Elected from SGB members by SGB councilors; informs GSB 8 hours in advance; document production to representatives of 50 signatories; faculty members get documents within 24 hours

=== General Secretary ===
* '''SAC:''' GSC includes Joint Secretary; 5 members total
* '''SGB:''' GSC has no Joint Secretary; 4 members total

=== Academic Affairs ===
* '''SAC:''' Single Academic Affairs Secretary (AAS) for all students; AAC includes MCA representative
* '''SGB:''' Split into UGAS (undergraduate) and PGAS (postgraduate); Academic Council (AC) includes MBA representative; no MCA representative

=== Research Affairs ===
* '''SAC:''' Same basic structure; RAC includes MSc.Tech; RAS reports to SAC
* '''SGB:''' RC includes MSc, M.Tech; RAS reports to SGB; coordinates with UGAS/PGAS (not AAS)

=== Hostel Affairs ===
* '''SAC:''' HAS manages CMH (Maintenance), MC (Mess), GDSC (Gardens); is principal student representative in Hostel Management; 4 student representatives to Hostel Management specified
* '''SGB:''' HAS simply "follows guidelines specified by the hostel office"; no CMH/MC/GDSC structure; reports to SGB on hostel activities

=== Sports ===
* '''SAC:''' CSA has both Institute and Inter-hostel committees; interval between meetings for both
* '''SGB:''' Only CSA Institute mentioned; no separate Inter-hostel CSA

=== Campus Publications ===
* '''SAC:''' Full chapter (Chapter VI) with detailed editorial board appointment process, publication committee, dispute resolution, SAC oversight and censure powers
* '''SGB:''' Reduced to brief mention in Chapter II (Section 9); no dedicated chapter; no editorial board or publication committee details

=== Hostel Councils ===
* '''SAC:''' Full chapter (Chapter V) with detailed Hostel Council composition, 10 sections covering all secretaries, GBM procedures, reporting requirements
* '''SGB:''' No dedicated chapter on Hostel Councils; hostel matters briefly covered under HAS role

== Summary of Structural Philosophy ==

The SAC Constitution established a '''Westminster-style parliamentary model''' where:
* SAC (legislature) sets policy
* EC (executive) implements policy and is accountable to SAC
* GSB is the supreme authority with referendum power
* SAC has oversight, censure, and impeachment powers over EC

The SGB Constitution establishes a '''corporate governance model''' where:
* EC (including faculty) governs over SGB
* SGB proposes policies but EC reviews and approves
* DSW has final decision-making authority
* Student executives are accountable to SGB but operate under EC guidance
* Faculty presence is institutionalized in both SGB meetings and EC

== Deleted SAC Provisions ==

The following provisions from the SAC Constitution are absent in the SGB Constitution:

# '''Board of Students (Chapter III, Section N)''' — Student representatives to Board of Students
# '''Consultative Committee (Chapter II, Section 10)''' — Constitutional interpretation committee
# '''Interaction Chart (Chapter II, Section 11)''' — SAC interaction with official bodies
# '''Placement Council (Chapter IV, Section 8)''' — SPC and Placement Secretary
# '''Joint Secretary position''' — Removed from EC
# '''Chief Student Editor position''' — Removed from EC
# '''Campus Publications chapter''' — Reduced from full chapter to 2 sections
# '''Hostel Councils chapter''' — Entire chapter removed
# '''Hostel GBM provisions''' — Quorum (20%), extraordinary meetings (25% requisition), minutes distribution
# '''Senate approval requirement''' for amendments — Replaced by Director's approval

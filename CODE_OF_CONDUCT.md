# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the work, not the person. Do not make the project unsafe for the people it exists to help.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, then check the evidence.
- Critique claims, wording and sources — not contributors.
- Respect privacy. Do not ask for, or publish, personal data.
- Respect the right of reply.
- Accept correction gracefully, and correct quickly.
- Keep disagreement about the argument.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, doxxing, or coordinated pile-ons.
- Personal attacks, slurs, or comments about caste, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or appearance.
- Publishing private messages, contact details or documents without consent.
- Sexual attention or imagery in project spaces.
- Deliberately fabricated sources, quotes or screenshots.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commits, chat, and any space where you represent the project. It also applies to how you use the project's output: do not use these decks to harass a named person.

## Reporting

Report a conduct problem privately to a maintainer (see `GOVERNANCE.md`). Do not open a public issue about a person. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will:

1. acknowledge within 72 hours,
2. hear both sides where appropriate,
3. decide on a response,
4. record the decision without publishing personal details.

Possible responses: a private word, a request to edit, a temporary ban, a permanent ban, or removal of content.

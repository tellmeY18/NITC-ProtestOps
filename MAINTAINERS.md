## What a maintainer does

- reviews pull requests,
- runs the audit before a release,
- answers right-of-reply requests within 72 hours,
- keeps the claim tables current,
- says no to a claim that is not ready.

## Adding a maintainer

A new maintainer is added by consensus of the existing maintainers, after a few substantive contributions and a demonstrated commitment to `ETHICS.md` and `FACTCHECK.md`.

## Removing a maintainer

A maintainer is removed for inactivity (six months), for a serious breach of `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, or at their own request. Removal is recorded in the commit message, without personal details.

## Contact

Use GitHub issues for ordinary matters. For privacy, legal or conduct matters, use the private contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not put personal data in a public issue.

# Right of reply

If a deck names you, you have a right to be heard. This page explains how.

## Who can use it

Anyone named in a published deck, or anyone acting for them, can ask for a correction or submit a reply.

## What to send

- the post and panel number,
- the exact sentence,
- what you say is wrong,
- the document or fact that supports your position,
- whether you want a correction, a reply, or both.

You do not need a lawyer. A clear email is enough.

## What happens next

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 72 hours | We acknowledge. |
| 7 days | We check the claim against the source. |
| 14 days | We publish a correction and/or your reply, or explain why we are not changing it. |

## What we will publish

- **A factual correction** if the source does not support the claim. This is published in `outputs/<post>/CORRECTIONS.md` and, where possible, as a correction panel.
- **Your reply**, in full or as a fair summary, if it is relevant to the claim. We may edit for length or clarity, but not for meaning, and we will show you the edit before publishing.
- **A note** that the claim is disputed, if the evidence is genuinely ambiguous.

## What we will not do

- We will not remove criticism because you disagree with it.
- We will not remove a claim that is supported by the document.
- We will not publish a reply that contains personal data about a third party, a threat, or a statement we know to be false.
- We will not negotiate in private and then deny the exchange. The correction log is public.

## How to submit

Open an issue titled `Right of reply: <post> / <panel>` **without personal details**, or use the private contact in `GOVERNANCE.md`. If the matter is sensitive, use the private route.

## The principle

A right of reply is not a concession to power. It is what makes the criticism credible. A claim that can survive a reply is stronger than a claim that was never tested.

## For maintainers

1. Log the request (date, post, panel, claim) without the requester's personal details in the public repo.
2. Check the source. Do not accept the reply just because it is angry, and do not reject it just because it is inconvenient.
3. If the claim is wrong, correct it visibly. Do not silently edit.
4. If the claim is right, say so, publish the reply, and explain the basis.
5. Record the outcome in `CORRECTIONS.md`.
6. If the request is a legal notice, follow `docs/legal-and-licensing.md` §11.

# Governance

This is a small project. The governance should be smaller than the project.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are the people with commit access. They are listed in `MAINTAINERS.md` (create it when the project has more than one). The founding maintainer is the person who published the first deck.

Maintainers are responsible for:

- merging changes,
- running the audit before a release,
- answering right-of-reply requests,
- keeping the claim tables current,
- saying no to a claim that is not ready.

## Decisions

- **Routine changes** (docs, tools, templates, typo fixes): one maintainer review.
- **Published claims and new decks:** two maintainer reviews, or one maintainer plus the person who gathered the evidence.
- **Ethics, licence and governance changes:** rough consensus of maintainers, with a public issue and a comment period of at least seven days.
- **Removing a person's name or personal data:** any maintainer can act immediately, and reports the action afterwards.

If there is no consensus, the maintainers decide by simple majority. If the vote is tied, the change does not happen. Doing nothing is a valid outcome.

## Disagreement

Disagree in the open, about the work, not the person. If you think a claim is wrong:

1. Quote the sentence.
2. Quote the source.
3. Say what the source actually supports.
4. Propose the weaker wording.

"That's biased" is not a review. "The minute says 'considered', not 'approved', so the slide overstates it" is.

## Conflicts of interest

If you are named in a deck, or you are close to someone who is, say so and let another maintainer review the change. If you are a member of the body being criticised, you can still contribute — but disclose it.

## Right of reply

Any person named in a deck may submit a reply. Maintainers must:

- acknowledge within 72 hours,
- check the reply against the source,
- publish a correction if the reply shows an error,
- publish the reply, in full or as a fair summary, if it is relevant,
- record the exchange in `outputs/<post>/CORRECTIONS.md`.

A reply is not a veto. It is a right to be heard, not a right to delete criticism.

## Removing content

Content is removed when it:

- contains personal data,
- names a private individual,
- is factually wrong and cannot be corrected,
- creates a credible risk to someone's safety,
- infringes copyright.

Content is not removed because it is uncomfortable, unpopular, or the subject dislikes it.

## Money

This project does not take money. No ads, no sponsors, no paid placements. If that changes, it will be a public decision with a public ledger.

## Licence

The repository is **GPL-3.0-or-later**. Contributions are accepted under the same licence. The licence can only change with the consensus process above, and never retroactively.

## Forking

Fork it. That is what the licence is for. If you fork, change the name and do not imply endorsement. Keep the ethics file; it is the useful part.
